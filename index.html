Naturely Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Naturely ("the App") is a macOS application that provides natural ambient sounds for relaxation, sleep, focus, and personal use.

Data Collection

Naturely does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal information or other user data.

The App does not require an account or registration and does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

Audio and App Usage

Naturely plays audio sounds locally on your Mac. Your use of the App is not transmitted to us.

Third-Party Services

Naturely does not use third-party services for collecting or tracking user information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, the updated version will be made available at this URL.

Contact
